How it works

Features

Built for the road.
Not the app store.

📍

Live radar with distance

See every nearby vehicle as a colored dot with real-time distance in feet. No map. No clutter. Just what matters.

⚡

Speed-scaled alerts

Your alert radius automatically increases at higher speeds — giving you more reaction time when you need it most.

📵

Works in the background

Proxar runs quietly using GPS to detect nearby vehicles in real time. When it matters most — you'll know.

🆘

Help / SOS mode

Stranded on the side of the road? Describe your situation in 50 words and nearby drivers are instantly notified.

🔊

Custom alerts per mode

Set different sounds for each vehicle type. Emergency alerts are always on and cannot be silenced.

🌙

Light and dark mode

Proxar adapts to your system appearance. The dark radar interface is designed for night driving.
